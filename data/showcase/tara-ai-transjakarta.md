## What is Tara AI?

**Tara AI** is an intelligent assistant built for **Transjakarta** to help operational teams and commuters get faster, data-informed answers about Jakarta's BRT network. The project applies natural language processing and machine learning to surface relevant transit information and support smart mobility workflows inside one of Southeast Asia's largest bus rapid transit systems.

## Key Capabilities

- **Operational support** — Helps teams respond to recurring transit questions with consistent, context-aware answers drawn from operational data.
- **Commuter assistance** — Surfaces route, schedule, and service information so passengers can plan trips with less friction.
- **NLP pipeline** — Processes natural-language queries and maps them to the right transit context instead of rigid keyword matching.
- **API-first design** — Built with FastAPI so the assistant can integrate with internal tools and future Transjakarta digital channels.

## My Role

As **AI Engineer Intern at Transjakarta**, I contribute to developing ML and NLP solutions for smart mobility — translating operational needs into data-driven tools that support Jakarta's public transportation ecosystem. Tara AI is part of that work: connecting transit data, language understanding, and real-world commuter and operator workflows.

## Why It Matters

Public transportation in a megacity like Jakarta depends on timely, accurate information at scale. Tara AI is designed to reduce manual lookup time for teams and give commuters a clearer path through the network — aligning AI engineering with the day-to-day reality of urban mobility.
